# Scoring sheet — ISO/IEC 25010 instruments

## Likert scale

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Strongly Disagree |
| 2 | Disagree |
| 3 | Neutral |
| 4 | Agree |
| 5 | Strongly Agree |

## Characteristic means

For each characteristic, compute:

\[
\bar{x}_c = \frac{\sum \text{item scores in characteristic } c}{n_c}
\]

Optionally report sample SD. Exclude blank “N/A” responses from both sum and \(n_c\).
